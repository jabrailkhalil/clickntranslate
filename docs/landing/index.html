FAQ

A few things to know.

Does it work offline?+

Yes. Download the recognition languages and an Argos Translate or Hy-MT model first. Online translation providers still need an internet connection.

How do updates work?+

On Windows, use the Update button in the app. On Mac and Linux, download the new DMG or AppImage and replace the app, keeping your user data.

Does it work in every game?+

It can read visible text in games and videos. Accuracy depends on the font and image quality. Borderless or windowed mode may be needed; capture restrictions can prevent access.

Can I change the shortcuts?+

Yes, in Settings. On Mac, use Command instead of Ctrl and Option instead of Alt. On Linux, configure the shortcuts in your desktop settings. The companion also gives you access to the actions.

What about documents and Mac permissions?+

Open TXT, Markdown, DOCX, PDF, HTML or RTF files. On Mac, the app guides you to Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions. See the setup guides beside the downloads.

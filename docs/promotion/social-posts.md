## LinkedIn — product and technical angle

Text inside a game, picture or app is not always selectable. Click’n’Translate
turns that screen content into text you can copy or translate.

The desktop edition provides region/full-screen capture, selected-text
replacement, changing-region overlays and document workspaces. Android has a
floating capture button plus text, photo and Share workflows. Windows, macOS,
Linux and Android packages are available from the project site.

Users choose local engines, online services or their own API connections.
Online text services receive text; an enabled image API receives the image too.
Supported offline models process locally after installation.

The project is free and open source. I welcome reproducible samples and feedback
on first launch, OCR and accessibility: https://translate.clickn.dev/

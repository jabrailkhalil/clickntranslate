## LinkedIn — product story

Text on a screen is not always usable text. In games, videos, images, remote
desktops, and older applications, it may be impossible to select or copy.

I built Click'n'Translate to remove that interruption. A global hotkey can copy
text from any screen area with OCR, translate a region or the full screen,
translate or replace selected text, or keep several changing regions translated
in place. The same application also includes a document workspace.

Users can choose between four OCR engines and either online or optional offline
translation. The privacy boundary is explicit: OCR is local; installed
Argos/Hy-MT translation is local; an online provider receives the text requested
for translation when selected.

The project has now passed 1,000 downloads. It remains free, requires no account,
and is open under GPL-3.0 for Windows and Linux.

Source and downloads: https://github.com/jabrailkhalil/clickntranslate

I am looking for concrete feedback on difficult fonts, languages, Linux desktop
environments, and first-run usability.
